INFLOW design system v1 · dark · 2026-10-10
ONE-PAGE SUMMARY
Rename Storm Chasers to INFLOW: the warm, moist air that feeds a storm. Here, every feed flows into one area of interest.
It's short and calm. It's a real meteorology word that enthusiasts know, and it's nothing like the TV series or any NOAA/NWS mark. Do a clearance search before launch (USPTO class 9/42 and app stores). See 01 Brand for the four alternatives.
THREE PRINCIPLES
P1Only hazards are saturated.The chrome is near-neutral graphite. Status colours are pale, low-chroma instrument tints. Only NWS hazard colours run at full saturation, so the loudest thing on screen is always an official warning, never us.
P2Form before colour.Solid means warning, dashed means watch, ringed and hatched means PDS, on the map and in the list alike. Each feed-health state has its own shape. A bordered element with a glyph is a control; a borderless fill is read-only.
P3One AOI, one focus, one accent.The AOI chip drives every panel. The ice accent means only one thing: "you chose this". It marks the focused alert, the reticle around its polygon, the active control and the keyboard focus.

USAGE RULES
{{ r.g }}{{ r.t }}
VOICE + TONE
INFLOW speaks like a flight director on a calm channel: short, factual and present tense, with times always in Z. Console labels are UPPERCASE nouns of two or three words with no verbs or punctuation (NWS ALERTS, DOT CAMERAS), and codes like WX-02 are fixed identifiers, not decoration. Body copy is sentence case. It says what is true, then what you can do, and stops. We never write hype, exclamation marks or emoji, and we never use "alert", "watch" or "warning" for anything the NWS didn't issue. A chaser's report is a "report", and an unconfirmed one says so. Empty states name the absence in the label (NO SCANNER SYSTEMS IN AOI), give one sentence of why or when, and offer at most one action.

ACCENT · JUDGEMENT CALL
#8DC5EE
Chosen: Ice, oklch(0.80 0.08 238). No hue is fully clear of radar, IR and SPC, so the accent stays clear by chroma, lightness and form. Radar blues are fully saturated (chroma above 0.18) and show only weak echo, while Ice is pale and low-chroma. On the map it is never a fill, only a 2 px stroke or bracket with dark casing.
Rejected: the current cyan #36C5F0 (it matches the 5–15 dBZ reflectivity cyan); copper/amber (reads as SVR orange or TOA yellow); violet (collides with the SVA watch, IR cold-top purples and SPC HIGH magenta); teal/mint (radar green, and the old live colour).
